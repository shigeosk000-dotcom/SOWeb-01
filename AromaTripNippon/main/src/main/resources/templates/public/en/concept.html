和香巡りとは

A journey carried by scent.

Aroma Trip Nippon is a fragrance-making experience for travelers who want to meet Japan through regional botanicals, quiet stories, and personal memory.

The English site keeps the same layout, color palette, and visual rhythm as the Japanese site, while making the service easy to understand and book for international guests.

Fragrance as a travel memory

A scent you create, a journey you can take home.

Guests learn about Japanese ingredients, compare scent profiles, and blend a personal fragrance that reflects their trip.

Designed for English-speaking guests

Navigation, explanations, form labels, validation guidance, and completion details are available in English, while the reservation is stored in the same management system.

Regional stories

The experience presents places such as Noto, Tosa, Kanazawa, and Edo not as tourist labels, but as scent stories expressed through woods, citrus, herbs, and cultural memory.
